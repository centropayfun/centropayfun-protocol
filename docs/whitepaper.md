# Centropay Whitepaper

## 1. Vision and mission

**Vision.** Digital assets should be as easy to spend as the money already in a pocket.

**Mission.** Build a single-hop route from a Solana wallet to a payment card: fast, transparent, and with no markup added by Centropay.

Targets, stated as goals and not as guarantees:

- Conversions that can complete in as little as two seconds.
- A Centropay fee of 0%. Network costs and third-party charges may still apply.
- One step between wallet and card.

**Phase 3 — Rails.** Pilot of wallet-to-card conversions with a small group; tuning toward the two-second target; end-to-end check of the zero-fee line.
